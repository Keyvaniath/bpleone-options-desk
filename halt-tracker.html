🧠 How to trade halts
LULD halt (5-min)
Limit-Up Limit-Down trip. Volatility-driven. Often re-opens near halt price. Tape on reopen tells direction.
T1 news pause
News-pending. Stock reopens after exchange dissemination (15-30 min). Often big gap. Avoid pre-reopen; trade the reaction.
T12 regulatory halt
SEC/FINRA action. Can last days/weeks. Reopens often massively gapped. Avoid as primary entry — too binary.
