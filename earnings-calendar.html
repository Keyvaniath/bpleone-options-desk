📊 Implied Move vs Realized — Last 4 Quarters
Implied is what straddle prices say the move will be. Realized is the actual after-print move. Realized < Implied = IV was over-priced (favor premium selling structures: iron condors, strangles short). Above the line = directional plays were rewarded.
🎯 Strategy Recommendations
📞 Long call if you think they beat
Buy slightly OTM (0.30-0.35 delta) calls expiring 2-3 weeks out. Limits IV crush damage if right direction but small move.
↕️ Long straddle if you expect a big move (either way)
Buy ATM call + put. Requires move > implied to profit. IV crush will hurt — exit quickly after print.
🦋 Iron condor if you expect a small move
Sell IV before earnings, profit from crush + theta. Wings at 1.5x implied move on each side.
⚠ Avoid: long single-leg calls into earnings
Most common rookie mistake. Even if you're right on direction, IV crush can wipe out gains. Use spreads instead.
