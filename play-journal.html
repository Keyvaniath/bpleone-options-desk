How this works. Cards you log on Strikes & Tranches land here, private to this browser (nothing uploads; Export saves a JSON backup). Each open card is marked against the ~15-min-delayed chain, and when a pre-committed rule triggers — target, stop, invalidation, time stop — the card lights up. The journal doesn't trade for you: it reminds you what you promised yourself. Discipline is the product; direction was never proven (scorecard).
