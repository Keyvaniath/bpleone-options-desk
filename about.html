Who's running this

A research analyst's eye for a retail trader's wallet.

I spent years on the institutional side covering equities — building models, dissecting earnings, reading the tape. The truth is most of what professionals do isn't magic. It's discipline, repetition, and a process for filtering noise. The problem is the retail world is buried in noise: paid Discords, fake gurus, screenshots of cherry-picked winners.

This site is my answer. Every day I publish a single trade of the day — the one I'd take with my own money, with the thesis, levels, sizing, and the options contract spelled out. I publish the wins and the losses, tagged and timestamped. The Education Hub is what I wish I'd had when I started.

Built in Southern California. Powered by too much coffee, a few Bloomberg muscle memories, and the belief that retail traders deserve better tools than what's currently being sold to them.

Frequently Asked

Is this financial advice?
No. Everything published here is educational and informational. I'm not a registered investment adviser, and what I post is what I'm watching for myself — not a recommendation for your account.
Do you actually take these trades?
When the setup matches my own portfolio rules and risk tolerance, yes. Sometimes I size smaller, sometimes I skip due to portfolio concentration. The track record reflects the published model, not my personal P&L.
What data sources do you use?
A combination of broker data, an options flow data provider, and my own scanner code. The website displays representative figures — production deployment connects to live feeds.
Is this really free?
Yes — every page, every feature, no credit card. We're in open beta and growing the user base. You bring your own (free) Finnhub key and (optional) Anthropic key; we don't charge you anything.
Will paid tiers come back?
Eventually, but beta subscribers will be grandfathered into the lowest tier permanently. Join the email list to lock that in.
