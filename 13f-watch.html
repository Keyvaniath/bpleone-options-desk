Read this before acting on a 13F. A 13F is a snapshot of a fund's US long positions as of quarter end, published up to 45 days later — the fund may have exited or reversed every position since. It omits shorts, options hedges context, and non-US holdings, so a headline position can be one leg of a trade you can't see. A filing appearing here is a fact, not an endorsement, and this desk has no proven edge acting on any of it (see the live scorecard). Open the EDGAR link and read the actual holdings table before drawing conclusions.
