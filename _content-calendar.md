# 2-Week launch content calendar — bpleone / trade

Goal: after the launch thread, keep a steady, **honest** drumbeat so the feed isn't a ghost town. Every post draws on the desk's REAL output (the pick, the scorecard, insider/congress flow, the options chain) or teaches the methodology. Nothing fabricated; every number must match what a visitor sees on the site that day.

**Cadence:** 1 post per weekday (10 posts). Markets-hours-aware: pick/market posts in the morning ET; education/build-in-public posts midday. Skip weekends or queue a light "week ahead" note.

**The golden rule:** if you can't link it to a live page on the site, don't claim it. The whole brand is "the numbers are real and you can check them."

---

## WEEK 1

**Mon — LAUNCH.** Post the X launch thread (see `_x-launch-thread.md`). Pin tweet 1. Post the LinkedIn version same day.

**Tue — Establish the daily rhythm: "Pick of the Day."**
> Today's highest-conviction call from the brain: $[SYM], [LONG/SHORT] at [conv]% conviction over a 5-day horizon. Honest edge status: [mostly drift / measured edge]. Not advice — here's the full reasoning + the live record → [pick-of-day link]
*(Pull the real pick from the site. If conviction is low / "no clear edge today," POST THAT — "Brain says no clear setup today. That's a feature." It builds trust faster than a forced call.)*

**Wed — Methodology explainer: the drift bar (mini-thread).**
> Why I don't say my model "beats a coin flip" 🧵
> Stocks drift up over time — a coin flip already wins >50%. So "55% accuracy" means nothing on its own. The only honest bar is: do you beat the *drift base rate*, at 95% confidence? Most tools fail this quietly. Mine reports it either way → [proof link]
*(Evergreen. Can be re-run monthly with fresh numbers.)*

**Thu — "What the brain got wrong." (the differentiator)**
> Accountability post: the brain called $[SYM] [direction] last week. It was wrong — [what happened]. Here's why, and what the calibration says about trusting that conviction band → [proof/edge-scorecard link]
*(Nobody posts their misses. This is the single most trust-building format you have. Run it weekly.)*

**Fri — Week-in-review scorecard.**
> Week 1 scorecard, graded honestly:
> • [N] predictions resolved
> • [X]% directional vs [Y]% drift base rate
> • Edge over drift: [+Zpp / not significant yet]
> No spin. The running record updates live → [money-made link]

## EVERGREEN FORMAT BANK (reuse anytime the calendar runs dry)
1. **Pick of the Day** — the real daily call + honest status.
2. **The miss post** — a wrong call, owned and explained.
3. **Scorecard update** — graded count vs drift (weekly).
4. **Methodology micro-thread** — one concept (BSS, calibration, drift bar, walk-forward) explained simply.
5. **Insider/congress highlight** — a real interesting disclosure.
6. **Options/vol note** — an observation from the live chain.
7. **"No setup today"** — when conviction is low. Counterintuitively great for trust.
8. **Behind the build** — a technical/architecture post (worker, self-grading, caching).
9. **Myth-bust** — quote a hype-y "AI trading" claim and contrast with what honest evaluation looks like.
10. **Calendar look-ahead** — real catalysts (earnings/econ) from the site, framed as "what I'm watching."

## RULES
- Every number must match the live site that day. Pull fresh before posting.
- Never sell signals, never promise profit, never claim an edge you can't link to the proof page.
- Post as Brandon (face + name). The honesty angle dies as an anonymous bot.
- 1 cashtag max; no cashtag spam.
- When in doubt, post the *honest* version — the miss, the null result, the "no setup." That's the moat.
