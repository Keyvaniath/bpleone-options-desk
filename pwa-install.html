📋 If the button didn't work — install manually
Chrome / Edge / Brave (desktop)
1
Click the install icon (⬇ or ⊞) on the right side of the URL bar.
2
Confirm "Install bpleone / trade" in the popup.
3
The app opens in its own window with a home-screen icon. Done.
Safari (iPhone / iPad)
1
Tap the Share button (□↑) in the bottom bar.
2
Scroll down + tap "Add to Home Screen".
3
Tap "Add" in the top right. Icon appears on home screen.
Chrome (Android)
1
Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
2
Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
3
Confirm. Done.
