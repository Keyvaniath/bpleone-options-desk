🎯 How this is built
The smart watchlist re-scans every brain finding from the last 6 hours, deduplicates by symbol (keeping the highest ML confidence per symbol), and tier-ranks them:
A-tier (≥80% ML confidence): Trade these with full size. The model has high conviction.
B-tier (65-80%): Take half-size or use them as paper-trade learning.
C-tier (50-65%): Watch only — too marginal to act on.
Anything below 50% is filtered out.
