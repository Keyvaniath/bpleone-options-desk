📚 Why aggregate to sector level
Per-symbol BSS (symbol-skill.html) tells you about each individual ticker. But sometimes you want the bigger picture: is the brain good at TECH or BAD at it?

This page rolls up symbol resolutions into sector buckets so:
  • A new symbol added to a strong sector starts with confidence — the brain has demonstrated edge in that category
  • If crypto sector shows -BSS but semi shows +BSS, you might restrict the brain to semi trades only
  • Sector multipliers (planned wire into UnifiedPredictor) would compound with the existing hourly/dow multipliers
Per-symbol Sharpe and per-sector Sharpe together give you a complete view of where the brain's profitable edge actually lives.
