🧠 How versioning works
Every time you click Snapshot Current (or run a Full Retrain), the model's weights are frozen and saved as a version. Snapshots are kept in localStorage with up to 50 most-recent. Promote any past snapshot to current with one click — useful for rollback after a bad training run. Compare any two versions side-by-side to see weight drift and accuracy differences.
