💡 What this means
A coin flip is the weak bar: in a rising market an always-long call is right more than half the time, so a hit rate above 50% can be pure drift. This test uses the drift rate (the share an always-long call got right on the same calls) as the null.
The z-score and p-value say how unlikely the observed hit rate is if the model were no better than drift.

p < 0.05: unlikely to be drift alone at this sample.
p > 0.10: indistinguishable from drift.

Daily calls on overlapping 5-day windows are not independent, so the real evidence is weaker than this p-value suggests (the Worker's tests correct for that). The desk's live record is the Edge Scorecard.
