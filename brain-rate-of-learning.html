💡 Interpretation
Slope > 0: The rolling hit rate is trending up. That is learning only if it ends above the drift line (an always-long call on the same days).
Slope ≈ 0: Plateaued. Either you hit your ceiling, or the model needs a refresh / new features.
Slope < 0: Degrading. Possible regime change. Investigate drift, consider retraining or pausing.

A hit rate above 50% is not skill: in a rising market an always-long call beats a coin flip. The bar is the drift line. The desk's live record is the Edge Scorecard.
