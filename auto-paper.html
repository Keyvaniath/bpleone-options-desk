🧠 How autopilot works
When ON, this page auto-opens a paper position every time the brain emits a finding meeting:
  • Severity ≥ medium (2-star or higher)
  • Model probability ≥ your threshold (default 70%)
  • No existing position on that symbol
When the brain rates the outcome (30 min after firing), the position auto-closes. Wins = +risk × win-R, losses = −risk × loss-R. Pure model performance — no human discretion in the loop. This is the cleanest test of model edge.
