📌 Disclosure rules + edge
Members of Congress + spouses must disclose trades ≥$1k under the STOCK Act. Window: 45 days. Returns from following high-conviction trades have averaged ~7% alpha annually in academic studies, with biggest edge in members on relevant committees (financials, defense, tech) trading those sectors. Late disclosures + bunched trades = informed positioning likely.
