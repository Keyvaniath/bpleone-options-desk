🧠 How it works
Watchlists are stored in your localStorage and synced to URL parameters when shared. Share the URL — anyone opening it gets the list pre-loaded in their Watchlist PRO. No backend; no signups; the data lives in the URL itself (Base64 encoded). Up to ~50 symbols per list before URL gets unwieldy.
