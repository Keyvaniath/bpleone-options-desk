💡 About notifications
The brain fires a desktop notification when a finding has high severity AND model confidence ≥ 78%. There's a 30-minute cooldown per symbol so you don't get spammed. Every fire is logged here. If a symbol is generating too many notifications, click Mute — it won't fire again until you unmute, even if it hits the threshold. Replay re-fires the notification so you can verify your notification settings work.
