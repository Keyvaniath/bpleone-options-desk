Why anti-overconfidence? Even a brain with positive edge will hit hot streaks just by chance. Continuing to size up after 7 wins ignores the fact that the next outcome is conditionally independent of the streak — but the trader's position size often isn't. Sizing down slightly during streaks protects against the mean reversion that always comes eventually.
