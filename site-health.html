📚 How to read this page
Each row above is one diagnostic. Green = brain is doing what it's supposed to in this dimension. Yellow = warning. Red = something concrete you should investigate.

TradeTrust ≥ 75 → safe to trade automated. 60–74 → reduce size. < 60 → paper-only. < 40 → AutoPause fires.
Brier Skill > 0.10 = the brain genuinely beats baseline. < 0 = brain is anti-informative (rare, would mean something is corrupting the labels).
Annual Sharpe > 1 = professional grade. > 2 = exceptional. < 0.5 = not worth running.
Drift PSI > 0.25 = major shift, brain should retrain. > 0.10 = minor shift, monitor.
Covariate shift |AUC − 0.5| > 0.20 = the input distribution looks different from the training distribution.
Per-symbol/sector shows where the brain has measurable edge. Negative = the brain is anti-informative on that name; consider excluding.
