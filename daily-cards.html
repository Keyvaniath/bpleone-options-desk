📆 Daily Cards
Auto-generated end-of-day brain summaries · browseable history day by day
📚 How it works
The brain auto-generates a daily card after market close (16:00 ET) capturing:
  • Brain health score + headline + alert count
  • Brier Skill Score + sample size
  • Annualized Sharpe + mean return
  • Current streak + win rate + total trades
  • Best / worst hour bucket
  • Top setup type
  • Drift + covariate-shift status
  • Trade trust score + active penalties
Cards are kept for the last 90 days. Click "📸 Snapshot now" to write a card from current state at any time during the day.
