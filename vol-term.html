Term Structure — today (real)
🎯 Signals
VIX — last ~40 sessions (30d implied)
The 30-day implied-vol gauge, real daily closes. Rising = fear bid; falling = calm.
SPY 20-day realized vol
Annualized stdev of SPY daily returns. Realized below implied (VIX) = vol risk premium (normal).
🧠 How to read the curve
Contango (VIX < VIX3M < VIX6M)
Normal regime. Vol-sellers profit from roll-down. Trend-followers thrive. Buy dips. VXX/UVXY decay aggressively.
Flat (within ±1 vol pt)
Transitional. Market repricing risk. Reduce conviction. Watch for sharp curve reshape — that's when vol regime breaks.
Backwardation (front > back)
Stress regime. Mean-reversion in equities works. Vol-sellers blow up. Hedges expensive but justified. Bottoms historically form here.
