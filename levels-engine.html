🧠 How levels are computed
Standard floor-trader pivots: Pivot = (H + L + C) / 3. R1 = 2P − L. R2 = P + (H − L). S1 = 2P − H. S2 = P − (H − L). These are statistical levels that institutional algos and bracket orders cluster around — meaningful for short-term S/R. Within 0.5% of any pivot triggers a "near" alert in this scan.
