🚨 Auto-Pause Circuit Breaker
When trade-trust drops below 40, brain auto-pauses · hysteresis prevents oscillation
⚙ Thresholds
Pause at trust < 40 — brain stops showing trade ideas on brain-bet
Cooldown at trust 40-60 — transitional state; brain doesn't show trades but is preparing to resume
Resume at trust ≥ 60 — full operation restored

The hysteresis (different threshold to pause vs resume) prevents oscillation when the score is right near the boundary.
📜 Transition history
📚 What it does
Polls TradeTrust every 60 seconds. When the score drops below 40, the circuit breaker fires:
  • Sets bpleone_auto_pause_v1.state = 'PAUSED' in localStorage
  • Fires a browser notification (if permitted)
  • Dispatches bpleone:auto-pause-change window event
  • Brain-bet checks AutoPause.isPaused() and shows "BRAIN PAUSED" instead of trade ideas
The state transitions are logged so Brandon can review what triggered each pause/resume.
