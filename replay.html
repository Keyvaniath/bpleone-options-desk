🧠 Training Workflow
1. Pick a day with a known move
Pick an FOMC day, a CPI day, or a stock-specific earnings day. Symbols that gapped hard are best for practice.
2. Run replay alongside paper trade
Open paper-trade.html in another tab. Replay drives the QUOTES; your paper trade fills against those prices.
3. Honest journal
Tag the trade as setup=replay-practice in journal. Replay trades shouldn't poison your real-money weights — keep them separate.
