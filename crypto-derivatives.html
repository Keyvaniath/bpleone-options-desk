🧠 How to read crypto derivatives
Funding > +30% annualized
Longs paying shorts heavily. Overheated positioning. Mean-revert / short squeeze setups fade. Watch for cascading long liquidations.
Basis spread > 10%
Cash-and-carry arb opportunity for institutions. Indicates strong directional conviction. Tail risk if basis collapses suddenly.
OI rising + funding flat
Healthy new money entering market. Spot-driven move. Bullish under-the-hood signal even in choppy tape.
