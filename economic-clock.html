🧠 How to trade around econ events
High-impact (FOMC, CPI, NFP)
Reduce positions 30 min before. First 5-min reaction often retraces 50%. Wait for 9:35-10:00 settled level.
Medium-impact (PCE, retail sales)
Tighten stops. Headline can spike intraday vol. Watch for fed-speaker follow-ups same week.
Setup play: pre-CPI hedges
5-day pre-CPI: buy 1-week SPY straddle when IV percentile <30. Avg post-CPI move was 1.4%.
