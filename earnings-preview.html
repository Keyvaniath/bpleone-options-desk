🎯 Strategy Recommendations
⚠ Earnings-Trade Risk Notes
Gamma risk is asymmetric
A pre-earnings short straddle can lose 3–5× the max gain if the stock outruns the implied move. Always use defined-risk structures (iron condors, fly spreads) unless you can hedge dynamically.
Liquidity gaps
Spreads in the after-hours session widen massively. If you trade the print, use limit orders only. Never use market orders into the close before earnings.
The post-earnings drift edge
Names that beat and gap up often continue drifting for 5–20 days as analyst PT hikes flow in. Conversely, gaps-down on miss continue. Position day 1 after the print is often better than playing the print itself.
