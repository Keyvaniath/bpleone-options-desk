🧠 How Plays Are Scored
Raw score combines (a) TA confluence — MA alignment, RSI position, MACD state, volume, pattern grade; (b) flow context — sweeps, blocks, dark-pool prints, call-put premium ratio; (c) momentum — multi-lookback RS vs SPY; and (d) vol regime — IV rank, term structure, gamma exposure.

Self-learning adjustment: each setup type carries a weight that's continuously updated from realized expectancy. After ~3 closed trades per setup, weights begin amplifying or dampening raw scores so the system rotates capital toward what's actually working in the current regime.
