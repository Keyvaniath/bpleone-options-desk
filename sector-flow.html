🧠 How to use sector rotation
Leading (top-right)
High RS + rising momentum. The winners. Trade longs here. Trim if RS momentum stalls.
Weakening (top-left)
High RS but falling momentum. Top of the cycle. Take profits, don't add. Watch for breakdown.
Lagging (bottom-left)
Low RS + falling. Avoid longs, watch shorts. Bear market for the sector. Bottoming candidates.
Improving (bottom-right)
Low RS but rising momentum. Early-cycle longs. Best risk/reward — new leadership often emerges here.
