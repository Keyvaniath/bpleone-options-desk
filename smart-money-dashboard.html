What this is (and isn't). Real public records: quarterly 13F filings (up to 45 days lagged, longs only), SEC Form-4 insider transactions (filed within 2 business days), and ~15-min-delayed daily options $-flow. It shows what these players did, not what you should do — none of it is a proven timing edge (live proof), and none of it is advice.
