🕯 Pattern dictionary
Hammer
Long lower wick + small body at top. Bullish reversal in downtrend. Best at support.
Shooting Star
Long upper wick + small body at bottom. Bearish reversal at resistance.
Bullish Engulfing
Green candle fully engulfs prior red body. Strong reversal signal at support.
Bearish Engulfing
Red candle engulfs prior green. Strong reversal at resistance.
Doji
Open ≈ close. Indecision. Context-dependent — direction confirmed by next bar.
Morning Star
3-bar pattern: down → doji → up. Strong reversal. Higher-probability than single-bar.
