🌐 Why track these.
These are US-listed ETFs, so they trade during US hours: a country fund prices its home session after the fact, it does not lead the US open. Currencies (dollar, euro, yen) move commodity and tech rotation, Treasury prices move growth-vs-value, and VXX tracks short-dated VIX futures. RSI, the 1-month change, the distance from the 50-day average and the 52-week position are computed from each fund’s daily closes.
