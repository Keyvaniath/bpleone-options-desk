🧠 Why $ volume beats share volume
Share volume is meaningless when comparing $500 BRK to a $5 penny stock. $ volume = price × shares = actual capital flowing through. It tells you where institutions are really active. A 30M-share day on NVDA = ~$4B of capital. A 30M-share day on SOFI = ~$400M. Same headline number, 10× different significance.
