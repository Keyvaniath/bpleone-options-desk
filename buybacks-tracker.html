🧠 Why buybacks matter
Aggressive (≥70% executed)
Company actively buying — provides daily flow demand. Floors price during selloffs. Often signal of management confidence.
Large program ≥5% of cap
Meaningful share reduction. Mechanically boosts EPS. Multi-year tailwind for stock if company executes consistently.
Slow / paused programs
Watch for cash strain or capital reallocation. Companies pause buybacks before bad news ~30% of time. Signal not noise.
